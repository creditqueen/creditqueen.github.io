Top 10 0 APR Business Credit Cards to Save Money

Running a business in today’s economy is no easy feat. With inflation, supply chain disruptions, and rising interest rates, every dollar counts. One of the smartest ways to manage cash flow and reduce financial strain is by leveraging a 0% APR business credit card. These cards offer an interest-free period, allowing you to finance expenses, invest in growth, or cover unexpected costs without paying extra in interest.

Here’s a curated list of the top 10 0 APR business credit cards to help your company save money while navigating today’s financial challenges.

Why a 0% APR Business Credit Card Matters in 2024

The Rising Cost of Borrowing

With the Federal Reserve maintaining higher interest rates to combat inflation, traditional loans and credit lines have become more expensive. A 0 APR business credit card provides a temporary reprieve, giving entrepreneurs breathing room to allocate funds strategically.

Cash Flow Management

Small businesses often face uneven revenue streams. A 0% intro APR period (typically 12–18 months) allows you to smooth out cash flow gaps without incurring debt from high-interest loans.

Funding Growth Without Debt

Whether it’s upgrading equipment, hiring staff, or launching a marketing campaign, a 0 APR card lets you invest in your business’s future without immediate financial pressure.

Top 10 0 APR Business Credit Cards

1. Chase Ink Business Unlimited®

  • 0% APR: 12 months
  • Rewards: 1.5% cash back on all purchases
  • Annual Fee: $0
  • Best For: Flat-rate cash back with a long intro APR period

Why It Stands Out:
No annual fee and unlimited 1.5% cash back make this card ideal for businesses that want simplicity and savings.

2. Bank of America® Business Advantage Customized Cash Rewards

  • 0% APR: 9 months
  • Rewards: 3% cash back in a category of your choice (up to $50,000 per year)
  • Annual Fee: $0
  • Best For: Flexible cash back options

Why It Stands Out:
The ability to choose your 3% category (like travel or office supplies) adds versatility.

3. American Express Blue Business Cash™

  • 0% APR: 12 months
  • Rewards: 2% cash back on up to $50,000 per year, then 1%
  • Annual Fee: $0
  • Best For: Steady cash back with no category restrictions

Why It Stands Out:
Amex’s robust customer service and expense management tools are a bonus.

4. Wells Fargo Business Platinum

  • 0% APR: 9 months
  • Rewards: None (purely a 0 APR card)
  • Annual Fee: $0
  • Best For: Businesses that prioritize interest savings over rewards

Why It Stands Out:
A straightforward option for those who just need a temporary 0% APR period.

5. U.S. Bank Business Platinum

  • 0% APR: 18 months (one of the longest)
  • Rewards: None
  • Annual Fee: $0
  • Best For: Long-term financing needs

Why It Stands Out:
The extended 0% APR window is perfect for large projects or slow-revenue cycles.

6. Capital One Spark Cash Select for Business

  • 0% APR: 12 months
  • Rewards: 1.5% cash back on everything
  • Annual Fee: $0
  • Best For: Simple rewards with no annual fee

Why It Stands Out:
Capital One’s user-friendly platform makes tracking expenses effortless.

7. CitiBusiness® / AAdvantage® Platinum Select® Mastercard®

  • 0% APR: 12 months
  • Rewards: 2x AAdvantage® miles on select business categories
  • Annual Fee: $99 (waived first year)
  • Best For: Frequent flyers

Why It Stands Out:
If your business involves travel, the airline perks can be invaluable.

8. Discover it® Business Card

  • 0% APR: 12 months
  • Rewards: 1.5% cash back (with first-year match)
  • Annual Fee: $0
  • Best For: Startups with low initial spending

Why It Stands Out:
Discover’s cash-back match at the end of the first year effectively doubles rewards.

9. PNC Cash Rewards® Visa Signature® Business Credit Card

  • 0% APR: 9 months
  • Rewards: 1.5% cash back
  • Annual Fee: $0
  • Best For: Regional businesses with PNC banking access

Why It Stands Out:
PNC’s business banking integration is seamless for existing customers.

10. BBVA Compass Business Secured Credit Card

  • 0% APR: 6 months
  • Rewards: 1% cash back
  • Annual Fee: $0
  • Best For: Businesses rebuilding credit

Why It Stands Out:
A secured card option for those who need credit improvement while still accessing 0% APR.

How to Maximize Your 0 APR Business Credit Card

Plan Your Spending

Map out major expenses during the 0% APR window to avoid interest later.

Avoid Late Payments

Missing payments can void the intro APR offer—set up autopay if possible.

Monitor the Regular APR

Once the 0% period ends, the rate jumps—plan to pay off the balance or transfer it.

Leverage Rewards Wisely

If your card offers cash back or points, use them to offset future expenses.

Final Thoughts

In an era where every financial decision impacts your bottom line, a 0 APR business credit card is a powerful tool. Whether you’re a startup or an established company, these cards provide flexibility, savings, and growth opportunities. Choose the one that aligns with your business needs, and use the interest-free period to your advantage.
